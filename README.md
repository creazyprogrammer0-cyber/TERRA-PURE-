# TERRA-PURE-
TERRA PURE - Agro-Processing &amp; Organic Foods


**Role & Objective:**
Act as a Principal UX/UI Designer with 10+ years of experience in B2B/B2C agricultural technology and organic consumer packaged goods (CPG). Design a high-converting, trust-oriented, modern web interface (Desktop 1440px) for an **Agro-Processing & Organic Foods** brand named **"TERRA PURE"**. The design must balance earthy, organic authenticity with industrial processing transparency, quality assurance, and global export readiness.

**Design System & Visual Hierarchy:**
- **Grid & Layout:** 12-column responsive grid, 1280px max-width container, 80px outer margins, 24px gutter. Use generous vertical section padding (96px–120px) to create breathing room and editorial clarity.
- **Surface & Elevation:** Use a warm, tactile organic paper/stone off-white background instead of stark clinical white. Use subtle 1px hairline borders (`#E2DFD7`) rather than heavy drop shadows to separate cards and data modules.
- **Corner Radius:** Soft, approachable geometry—12px for large containers/cards, 8px for buttons and inputs, 999px (pill) for certification badges and tags.

**Color Palette (Strict Hex Codes):**
- **Primary Brand (Deep Forest / Harvest Green):** `#1B4332` (Used for primary CTAs, headers, active states, and structural anchors)
- **Secondary Accent (Warm Terracotta / Raw Amber):** `#C86D51` (Used sparingly for secondary highlights, harvest badges, and key metric callouts)
- **Canvas Background (Warm Alabaster / Oat):** `#F9F8F4`
- **Card / Elevated Surface:** `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E5E2D9` border
- **Primary Text (Deep Soil Charcoal):** `#1E201E` (High contrast, never pure `#000000`)
- **Secondary Text (Muted Sage/Slate):** `#525B54`
- **Success / Organic Verified:** `#2D6A4F`

**Typography System:**
- **Headings (Editorial & Trustworthy):** `Fraunces` or `Playfair Display` (SemiBold, tight tracking `-0.02em`) for Hero H1 (56px/64px) and Section H2 (40px/48px) to evoke heritage, craft, and natural origin.
- **UI, Body & Data (Clean & Technical):** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` for H3 (24px), Body (16px/26px line-height), Navigation (14px Medium), and Processing Specs/Metrics (14px SemiBold, tabular figures).

**Page Architecture & Section-by-Section Instructions:**

1. **Top Navigation Bar:**
   - Sticky translucent glassmorphic header (`rgba(249, 248, 244, 0.9)` with backdrop-blur).
   - Left: Minimalist geometric leaf/grain wordmark logo ("TERRA PURE").
   - Center: Clean horizontal links (`Our Process`, `Organic Products`, `Traceability`, `Certifications`, `Wholesale & Export`).
   - Right: Language selector (`EN`), subtle search icon, and a solid `#1B4332` pill button (`Request Sample / Quote`).

2. **Hero Section (Split Editorial Layout):**
   - **Left Column (55%):** Small pill badge (`100% USDA & EU ORGANIC CERTIFIED`). Bold serif H1: *"From Living Soil to Pure, Cold-Processed Nutrition."* Subheadline explaining farm-to-facility cold-milling, dehydration, and zero-additive processing. Dual CTAs: Primary solid green (`Explore Product Catalog`) + Secondary outlined button (`Watch Facility Tour`). Below CTAs, include a clean horizontal trust strip showing key metrics: `45+ Partner Farms`, `0% Synthetic Additives`, `ISO 22000 & HACCP`.
   - **Right Column (45%):** High-resolution, sun-drenched editorial photograph of raw organic grains, cold-pressed oils, and fresh produce with a floating glassmorphic "Batch Traceability" UI card overlaid at the bottom-left showing live origin data (`Origin: Highland Valley | Moisture: 11.2% | Lab Tested: Passed`).

3. **Certification & Compliance Bar:**
   - Minimalist monochromatic logo strip on a subtle `#F2EFE9` band featuring clean vector icons/badges for: `USDA Organic`, `EU Organic`, `Non-GMO Project`, `Fair Trade Certified`, and `ISO 22000 Food Safety`.

4. **Agro-Processing Transparency (4-Step Interactive Process Cards):**
   - Section Header: *"Precision Processing That Preserves Nature."*
   - 4 equal-width cards (`#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E5E2D9`, 12px radius) numbered `01` to `04`:
     - `01. Regenerative Sourcing` (Direct-trade harvesting at peak nutrient density)
     - `02. Optical & Ultrasonic Cleaning` (Chemical-free impurity removal)
     - `03. Low-Temp Cold Processing` (Below 38°C milling & extraction to lock in enzymes)
     - `04. Nitrogen-Flushed Packaging` (Extended shelf-life without preservatives)
   - Each card includes a custom thin-stroke line icon in `#1B4332`, bold title, concise 2-line description, and a technical spec tag at the bottom.

5. **Featured Organic Product Categories (Bento Grid Layout):**
   - Asymmetrical 3-card Bento grid showcasing core product lines:
     - **Large Left Card:** *Cold-Pressed Botanical Oils* (Extra Virgin Mustard, Sesame, Flaxseed) with pack-size tags (`Bulk Drums` / `Retail Glass`).
     - **Top Right Card:** *Ancient Grains & Sprouted Flours* (Stone-ground Quinoa, Amaranth, Millet).
     - **Bottom Right Card:** *Dehydrated Superfood Powders* (Moringa, Turmeric, Freeze-Dried Berries).
   - Include clean product photography, hover-ready `View Spec Sheet` links, and origin tags.

6. **Farm-to-Fork Traceability Banner (Dark Mode Contrast Section):**
   - Full-width rounded container (16px radius) in Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`) with `#F9F8F4` text.
   - Left: *"Every Batch Tells Its Origin Story."* Explain QR-code batch tracking for retail buyers and bulk importers.
   - Right: Clean UI mockup of a mobile traceability scanner showing Soil Health Score, Harvest Date, Lab Aflatoxin/Pesticide Test (`0.00 ppm - Clear`), and Farmer Cooperative profile.

7. **B2B Bulk & Export Inquiry Section + Footer:**
   - Clean 2-column split: Left side lists bulk supply capabilities (Private Label / OEM, Custom Packaging, Global FOB/CIF Shipping). Right side features a streamlined, low-friction inquiry form card (Name, Company, Product Interest dropdown, Estimated Volume, Submit CTA).
   - Footer: Dark soil (`#161A17`) multi-column footer with newsletter signup, quality policy links, facility address, and copyright.

**Strict Design Rules (Negative Constraints):**
- NO generic neon greens (`#00FF00`), cheesy clip-art leaves, or outdated rustic wood-plank textures.
- NO cluttered text walls; keep card copy scannable (under 25 words per card).
- Maintain strict WCAG AA contrast ratios across all text and button states.
- Ensure consistent 24px grid alignment and clean visual breathing room throughout.
